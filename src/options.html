Open Autofill

Always free. Local only. Your answers never go to the cloud.

Privacy policy

Settings

Gmail, Outlook, and other webmail inboxes are skipped automatically so select-all boxes are left alone.

Identity profile

Used by default. If you enter a different email or phone on one site, that site-specific answer takes priority there.

Ignored sites

One hostname per line. Example: accounts.google.com

Remembered site fields

One-off questions and site-specific overrides live here, keyed to the website.

Backup

Export writes a JSON file to your computer. Import reads a file you choose. Neither step uploads anything.

Support

Open Autofill is free and stays on your computer. If it saves you time, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi.
