Open Autofill

Free local form memory for Chrome. Fills the fields you use again. No cloud. No daily limit.

Your answers stay in this browser profile. Open Autofill does not create accounts, sync to a server, or sell data.

  • Reusable identity fields (email, name, address, phone, social handles)
  • Per-site extras Chrome’s built-in autofill often misses
  • Always free. Local only.
